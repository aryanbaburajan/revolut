# Gunshot audio sources

- `pistol-shot.mp3`: supplied by the project owner.
- `rifle-shot.mp3`: supplied by the project owner.

The additional `p226-*` and `mp5-*` recordings are currently unused. They are edited recordings from The Free Firearm Sound Library by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, and Matthew Nanney, released under CC0 1.0:
https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library

Edited clips and provenance: https://github.com/yegors/hard-lines/blob/main/public/audio/README.md
